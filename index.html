Shadow Zoo

Make shadow puppets with your hands on your real wall, and watch them come alive.

  1. 1Sit facing a wall in a lit room and put your controllers down.
  2. 2Shape your hand until its shadow fits the glowing outline.
  3. 3Pinch for fireflies, rest your shadow to pet, and come back tomorrow.

Made for Meta Quest 3 and 3S. Hands only, seated.

Your zoo is saved only on this device. No accounts, no tracking.
