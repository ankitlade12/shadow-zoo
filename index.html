How it plays

A lantern behind you lights the wall you face. Your hands cast the shadows, and the shadows come alive.

Make the shape
Shape your hand until its shadow fits the glowing outline, and the creature wriggles free. Seven to find, from the rabbit to the bird that takes both hands.
Feed and pet
Pinch to release a firefly and your creatures chase it down. Rest your shadow on one to pet it; at three hearts it learns a trick.
Wear it
Make a creature you’ve found and become it. Snap the dog’s jaw and it barks.
Grab, throw, bonk
Pinch a creature to pick it up and let go mid-swing to throw it. Swipe fast to bonk the zoo, and clap to make everyone jump.
Travel the world
Pick a zoo on the globe above, or spin the globe on your wall from the Menu. Every place has its own light, weather, night sounds and rules.
Come back tomorrow
Your zoo remembers you, your streak warms the lantern, and a daily visitor brings a twist.

Try it anywhere

One link, nothing to install. The real game is made for a headset; a computer gets a preview.

On Meta Quest 3 or 3S

  1. Open this page in Meta Quest Browser.
  2. Sit facing a wall in a lit room and put your controllers down.
  3. Pick a zoo, press Light the lantern, and allow hand tracking and spatial data when asked.
  4. Make the rabbit (index and middle fingers up, the others folded) and it comes alive.

On a computer

  1. Open this page in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox: a preview of the game runs in Meta’s WebXR emulator, in a virtual living room.
  2. Pick a zoo and light the lantern with your real hands, held up to your camera.
  3. Or play with the mouse: keys 1 to 7 make the shapes, a click pinches, and Esc leaves.

Your camera picture stays on this device: only your hands’ shape is used, and nothing is recorded or sent.
